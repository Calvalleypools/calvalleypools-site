Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

Text messages

If you check the consent box on our quote form, Cal Valley Pool & Spa may text you about your quote request and service. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent isn't a condition of purchase.

We don't sell or share your phone number or SMS opt-in with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Texts are sent through our texting provider, Quo.

Google Ads

We use the Google Ads tag to measure when an ad leads to a quote request, and to show our ads to people who have visited our site. Google may use cookies or similar technology for this.

You can opt out at Google Ads Settings or at optout.aboutads.info.

Quote requests

The quote quiz collects your name, phone number, pool address, ZIP code, email address if you choose to give one, your pool's condition, and the services you ask about. Our website host, Netlify, stores that form. We also send it to our internal quoting tool so we can prepare a quote. We use it only to respond to your request and serve you as a customer.
