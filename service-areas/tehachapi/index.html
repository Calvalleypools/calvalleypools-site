POOLS IN TEHACHAPI

What Tehachapi pools are up against.

Pools in Tehachapi live in a different climate from pools down in Bakersfield. Winters are colder, nights can drop below freezing, and the swim season is shorter. Many pools sit unused for months and get less attention, and a pool that gets ignored in the off-season can be green by spring. The area is also known for strong wind, which blows leaves, dust and debris into the water and loads up skimmers and filters. Cold weather is hard on equipment too, so pumps, filters and plumbing are worth checking before you open the pool.

Cal Valley Pool & Spa is owner-operated by Brandon out of Bakersfield, and we’ve been in business around four years. The same tech who cleans your pool also fixes the equipment, so you deal with one person from the first call to the finished job.

WHAT WE DO IN TEHACHAPI

One-time pool services in Tehachapi.

Tile cleaning & bead blasting

Mineral scale and the dirt line that builds up over a long off-season make tile look tired. We bead blast the waterline tile to take off scale and stains so the tile looks clean again before swim season. More about tile cleaning ↗

Pool equipment repair

We work mostly on Pentair equipment: pumps, filters, heaters and automation. For pump replacements we install variable-speed pumps only. They run quieter and use less power than old single-speed pumps. More about pool repair ↗

Drain & clean / water change

After a long off-season, water that has collected debris and minerals is often easier to replace than to fix. A drain-and-clean gives you fresh water for the warm months. We plan the timing around Tehachapi weather so the pool isn't left empty in a freeze. More about water changes ↗

Green pool cleanup

Green, cloudy or swampy, we bring it back to clear, blue water. We find out why it turned (often a failed pump, a dirty filter or chlorine burned off by the heat) so it doesn’t happen again. More about green pool cleanup ↗
