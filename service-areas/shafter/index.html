POOLS IN SHAFTER

What Shafter pools are up against.

Pools in Shafter deal with the same things every Central Valley pool does, often a little harder. The water is hard, so calcium scale builds up along the waterline and on tile. Summer afternoons run hot for months, which burns off chlorine fast and makes algae grow quickly. And with open farmland around town, wind can carry dust, dirt and debris into the water, clouding it, clogging filters and feeding algae. A pool that looked fine on Friday can turn cloudy after a windy weekend.

Cal Valley Pool & Spa is owner-operated by Brandon out of Bakersfield, and we’ve been in business around four years. The same tech who cleans your pool also fixes the equipment, so you deal with one person from the first call to the finished job.

WHAT WE DO IN SHAFTER

One-time pool services in Shafter.

Tile cleaning & bead blasting

That white crust at the waterline is calcium, and in hard-water areas like Shafter it keeps coming back. Scrubbing rarely gets it off once it hardens. We bead blast the tile line to take the scale off without stripping the tile, so the tile looks clean again. More about tile cleaning ↗

Pool equipment repair

We work mostly on Pentair equipment: pumps, filters, heaters and automation. For pump replacements we install variable-speed pumps only. They run quieter and use less power than old single-speed pumps. More about pool repair ↗

Drain & clean / water change

When the water has taken on years of minerals and stabilizer, chemicals stop working the way they should. A drain-and-clean or water change resets the pool with fresh water, and it's a good time to clean the surface and tile while the pool is empty. More about water changes ↗

Green pool cleanup

Green, cloudy or swampy, we bring it back to clear, blue water. We find out why it turned (often a failed pump, a dirty filter or chlorine burned off by the heat) so it doesn’t happen again. More about green pool cleanup ↗
