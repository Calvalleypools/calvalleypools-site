WHAT WEEKLY SERVICE COVERS HERE

Landscaped yards, not the open west edge.

Leaves and landscape litter

Seven Oaks and the tracts around it are planted, established neighborhoods. Debris in the pool is usually leaves and landscape litter. Weekly service skims, brushes, vacuums and empties baskets as the weekly service page and your estimate describe them.

Water testing each visit

Southwest summers cook a pool the same way the rest of Bakersfield does. Each visit tests and balances the water so the chemistry is not a guess between weekends. Essential pool care tips cover what that balance is protecting.

Tile and equipment stay separate

Mineral buildup at the waterline is its own job. Tile cleaning and bead blasting are not folded into the weekly visit. Neither is pump, filter, heater or automation repair.

If the pool has gone green, that is a green-to-clean, scheduled on its own. The same is true for filter cleaning and a water change. The scope of work and price are confirmed with your estimate.

SEVEN OAKS IS NOT ROSEDALE

Where 93311 stops, and which names belong next door.

Riverlakes Ranch and the Villages of Brimhall are northwest, in 93312. The Rosedale expansion west of those tracts is 93314. 93311 is south of both. People usually mean the area around Ming Avenue, Old River Road, White Lane and Allen Road, with Seven Oaks Country Club at 2000 Grand Lakes Avenue, Bakersfield, CA 93311, as the landmark.

Haggin Oaks and The Oaks are often mentioned with 93311, but a street on the edge can be 93309 or 93312. The ZIP on the address decides the route. Weekly service is only 93306 (east Bakersfield), 93311, 93312 and 93314. An address in 93309 can still book repair, green-to-clean, filter cleaning, a water change or tile cleaning. It is not on the weekly route.
