A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you dive in.

What does every weekly visit include?

Each weekly visit includes water testing and chemical balancing, with chemicals included, plus skimming, brushing, vacuuming as needed, emptying baskets, and an equipment check.

Which areas get weekly pool service?

Weekly pool service covers 93311, 93312, 93314, and east 93306 only, toward Rio Bravo and Lake Ming. East 93306 means past Morning Dr and Hwy 178 toward Lake Ming, near Hwy 178 and Comanche, including Rio Bravo Country Club. The rest of 93306 is not on the weekly route.

Are chemicals included?

Yes. Chemicals are included with each weekly visit.

Are you insured?

Yes. Cal Valley Pool & Spa is insured.

What affects my weekly quote?

Pool size, whether a spa is attached, trees and debris around the pool, and the current condition of the pool. Call 661-747-9184 and describe the pool so the quote can be confirmed.

Can I get a repair outside the weekly area?

Yes. Pool repair and tile cleaning are available anywhere in Kern County, even outside the weekly route. Call 661-747-9184.
